BOTTLE LAKE BIKES — BRAND / DESIGN REFERENCE

EXISTING BRAND
The current Bottle Lake Bikes logo uses bold condensed/block typography in a very dark forest green on a light background.

PRIMARY BRAND DIRECTION
- deep Bottle-Lake-style forest green
- light / warm off-white background
- white
- dark charcoal/ink where useful
- optional restrained trail/sand neutral

IMPORTANT
Do not invent a new unrelated colour identity.
Before coding, inspect the existing Bottle Lake Bikes logo, local CSS/source and supplied assets and derive the EXACT existing brand green from those files.
Use that real colour value as the primary colour.

LOGO
The current public logo is a bold "BOTTLE LAKE BIKES" wordmark.
Use supplied local logo assets rather than rebuilding the logo from scratch if the asset exists.

DESIGN PERSONALITY FOR REDESIGN
- local Christchurch bike workshop
- confident but approachable
- strong workshop/trail photography
- modern without looking like a tech startup
- slightly outdoors / forest connected
- premium enough to build trust
- practical enough to feel like a real repair shop
- no generic cycling-race aesthetic
- no fake pro-team graphics
- no overuse of gears/cogs as decorative icons
- no neon sports-store look

FULL-SCREEN HERO
Use an existing high-quality image from the local assets folder.
Hero should immediately communicate bikes, local workshop/trail context and service.
Do not search for or download new imagery for this redesign unless explicitly requested later.

MOTION
Use a bike/workshop/trail-specific motion language:
- smooth full-screen hero image settle/scale
- horizontal track-like reveals
- subtle line motion suggesting trail paths / wheel movement without literal gimmicks
- restrained stagger on service content
- brand-logo/brands-page transitions that remain clean and product-focused

Do not use cartoon spinning wheels/gears.
Do not disable motion because of prefers-reduced-motion for this mockup.
