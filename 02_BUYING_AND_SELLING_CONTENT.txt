BOTTLE LAKE BIKES — BUYING & SELLING CONTENT

BUY A REFURBISHED BIKE
Bottle Lake Bikes sells good-quality, fully serviced bikes, giving customers confidence in the purchase while offering better value than buying new.

SELL US YOUR BIKE
Customers who want the simplest option can talk to Bottle Lake Bikes about selling the bike directly to the workshop.
This suits someone who wants to know the price upfront and avoid the hassle of selling privately.

LET BOTTLE LAKE BIKES SELL YOUR BIKE
Bottle Lake Bikes can sell a customer's bike on their behalf.
Before sale, the bike can be serviced, cleaned and polished to help achieve a stronger result.
This option suits owners who want to aim for a better sale result and are comfortable selling on commission.

CURRENT COMMISSION STRUCTURE
Sale price up to $600:
35% commission

Sale price $600 to $1,500:
30% commission

Sale price $1,500 to $3,000:
25% commission

Sale price over $3,000:
20% commission

Minimum commission:
$100

CURRENT NOTE
Bottle Lake Bikes provides an estimated sale price before listing a bike.
If the owner wants certainty about the amount they will receive and wants the money immediately, selling the bike directly to Bottle Lake Bikes is the alternative.
