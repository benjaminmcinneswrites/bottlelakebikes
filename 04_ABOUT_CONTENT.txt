- act as a buying agent for someone wanting a good used-bike deal with less risk

PATRICK'S BIKE STORY
Patrick's interest in bikes started around age 10.
He and his brother built backyard trails on their parents' farm.
They kept their bikes running using cheap second-hand parts and whatever tools they had available.
A memorable example from the current story is making a makeshift chain whip by tying an old chain to a workbench, and swapping a snapped frame on the garage floor.

INDUSTRY BACKGROUND
Patrick later worked in the cycling industry in roles including:
- service technician
- design engineer
- product sourcing

Starting Bottle Lake Bikes was the next step from that background.

GOAL / PHILOSOPHY

- care whether the job is a flat tyre or a full overhaul
- keep the customer informed
